# Pigeon Trichomonas & Candida Control

Crop and digestive health is the foundation of performance. **Trichomonas** and **Candida albicans** are the two most common digestive pathogens, often co-infecting.

## Pigeon Trichomonas

A protozoan that parasitizes the mouth, throat and crop:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Typical sign | Yellow-white plaques in mouth and throat |
| Digestive impact | Crop bloating, poor digestion, weight loss |
| Transmission | Passed from breeders to squabs via crop milk |
| High-risk group | Squabs, breeding breeders |

## Candida albicans

A fungus that proliferates when immunity drops or antibiotics are overused:

- **Typical sign**: crop inflammation, oral white plaques ("thrush")
- **Triggers**: prolonged or overuse of antibiotics, stress, poor nutrition

## Distinguishing the Two

| Feature | Trichomonas | Candida |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Pathogen | Protozoan | Fungus |
| Lesion | Yellow-white plaque, easy to strip | White plaque, harder to strip |

## FAQ

### Does Trichomonas recur?
Yes. It spreads repeatedly between breeders and squabs via crop milk. Regular testing and crop hygiene are key. See [virus detection](/en/virus-detection/).
