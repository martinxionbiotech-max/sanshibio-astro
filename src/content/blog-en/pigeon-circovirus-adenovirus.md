# Pigeon Circovirus & Adenovirus

Two "silent killers" among pigeon viruses — **circovirus** and **adenovirus** — often do not kill directly, but open the door to secondary infections by damaging immunity or digestion.

## Pigeon Circovirus (PiCV)

Circovirus attacks the immune system, causing **immunosuppression**:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Main harm | Destroys immunity, causes immunosuppression |
| Typical signs | Young pigeon disease, poor growth |
| Secondary infection | Bacteria, fungi and other viruses |
| High-risk group | Squabs, stressed birds |

## Pigeon Adenovirus (PiAd)

Adenovirus mainly attacks the liver and intestines, causing "young bird disease": vomiting, diarrhea, loss of appetite and weight loss.

## Why Early Detection Matters

Both viruses show vague early signs. [Sanshi Bio virus detection](/en/virus-detection/) uses **real-time PCR** to detect viral nucleic acid during the asymptomatic stage.

## FAQ

### Can circovirus-induced immunosuppression recover?
The virus is hard to eradicate, but reducing stress, improving nutrition and controlling secondary infections lowers its impact.
