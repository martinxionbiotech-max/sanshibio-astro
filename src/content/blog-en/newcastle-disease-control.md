# Racing Pigeon Newcastle Disease Control Guide

> **TL;DR**: Newcastle disease is among the most severe viral diseases in racing pigeons — highly contagious and often fatal. This guide covers virology, symptoms, spread and control, emphasizing early PCR detection.

**Newcastle disease (ND)** is one of the most serious viral infections in racing pigeons, caused by a paramyxovirus with high contagiousness and mortality.

## Pathogen & Transmission

| Route | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Respiratory | Aerosols from coughing and sneezing |
| Digestive | Contaminated water and feed |
| Contact | Direct contact with sick birds |
| Mechanical | Carried by people, equipment and vehicles |

## Clinical Signs

- **Respiratory**: rapid breathing, open-mouth breathing, coughing
- **Digestive**: green watery diarrhea, loss of appetite
- **Neurological**: head tilt, circling, wing droop, leg paralysis
- **Reproductive**: sudden drop in egg production

## Why Early Detection Matters

Newcastle disease spreads fast. [Sanshi Bio virus detection](/en/virus-detection/) uses **real-time PCR** to detect viral nucleic acid before obvious symptoms appear, enabling early isolation.

## FAQ

### Can Newcastle disease be cured?
No specific cure exists. Prevention (vaccination + biosecurity) is key. Early [detection](/en/virus-detection/) minimizes loss.

### How does Newcastle disease differ from avian influenza?
Different virus families (paramyxovirus vs orthomyxovirus) with overlapping symptoms but different control. Testing distinguishes them.

### Can vaccinated pigeons still be infected?
Yes — protection is not 100%. Check [antibody titer testing](/en/blog/antibody-titer-test/) and boost when titers drop.

## Key Takeaways

1. Caused by a paramyxovirus — highly contagious and highly lethal.
2. Three symptom clusters — respiratory, digestive and neurological signs.
3. Early detection is critical — qPCR detects the pathogen before symptoms appear.
4. Control = vaccination + biosecurity — standard immunization, pre-race screening, quarantine.
5. No specific cure — prevention first; isolate early to limit losses.
