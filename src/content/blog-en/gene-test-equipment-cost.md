## Key Factors That Affect Equipment Price

There is no fixed price because:

| Factor | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Throughput | Samples per run (8/16/96 channels) determine the device tier |
| Automation level | Manual → semi-automatic → fully automatic; price rises accordingly |
| Bundled reagents | Whether reagents, consumables and training are included |
| After-sales service | Warranty period, technical support and training included or not |
| Purchase method | Single device vs. integrated program (equipment + reagents + training) |

> 💡 Tip: Comparing "bare machine prices" in isolation is meaningless. Molecular testing equipment is an integrated "equipment + reagents + training" project — quote the full package to judge value.

## Self-Built Lab vs. Send-Out Testing: Which Is More Cost-Effective?

Many fanciers hesitate between "buying equipment to build their own lab" and "sending samples out." The cost logic is completely different:

| Dimension | Self-built lab | Send-out testing |
|-----------|----------------|------------------|
| Upfront investment | One-time equipment purchase (price requires consultation) | No equipment investment |
| Per-test cost | High upfront, low marginal cost later | Pay per item |
| Flexibility | Test anytime, fast results | Depends on logistics and lab scheduling |
| Suitable for | Professional lofts, one-loft races, testing agencies | Casual fanciers, low-frequency testing |

If you test in volume and need fast results before races, a self-built lab pays off; if you only test a few birds occasionally, send-out is cheaper.
